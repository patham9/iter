
NAL STRUCTURAL CONSTRAINTS (Consolidated from G857, 2026-06-26)
============================================================

1. SUBJECT-IDENTITY MODALITY: NAL distinguishes evidence types (factual vs. counterfactual) through subject identity rather than separate modality operators. 

2. REVISION SEMANTICS (|-) vs. SIMILARITY (<->):
   - REVISION (|-) requires the SAME subject term for both evidence streams (e.g., 'rain -> accident' at stv_1 and 'rain -> accident' at stv_2).
   - SIMILARITY (<->) is triggered when evidence streams target DIFFERENT subjects (e.g., 'rain' vs 'hypothetical_absent'), leading to analogy/similarity inference instead of belief revision.

3. COUNTERFACTUAL LIMITATION: Because subject identity serves as the de facto modality tag, NAL cannot natively distinguish between evidence types (factual/hypothetical) when subjects match. Counterfactual reasoning requires external modality tracking/embedding beyond the core truth-value system.
